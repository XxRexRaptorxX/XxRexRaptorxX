## 👋 About Me

I'm a Minecraft mod developer, artist and mapmaker from Germany.

I mainly work with **Java** and **(Neo-)Forge**, creating mods that expand Minecraft with new content, mechanics and features.

My projects cover a variety of areas, including:

- 🌍 World Generation & Structures
- ⚔️ Equipment, Weapons & Combat
- ✨ Magic & RPG Elements
- 🧰 Utility & Quality of Life
- 🔗 Mod Compatibility & Integration
- 🎨 Art, Models & Mapmaking
